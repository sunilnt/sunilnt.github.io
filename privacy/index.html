Privacy policy

How Royal Coat handles information sent through enquiries, events and business communication.

Effective 15 August 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy applies when you visit royalcoatworld.com or contact Royal Coat by email, telephone, WhatsApp, an event form or another business channel. Royal Coat is based at Damodar Haridas Building, T. H. Kataria Marg, Matunga (West), Mumbai 400016, India.

Information you choose to send

Depending on the enquiry, this can include your name, company, designation, country, telephone number, email address, the manufacturers or technologies you want information about, application details and any comments you provide. The website may also generate basic technical records through its hosting service, such as the time of a request and the type of browser used.

How Royal Coat uses the information

Royal Coat uses these details to answer enquiries, understand an application, arrange meetings, provide relevant technical or commercial information, follow up after an exhibition and maintain normal business records. Information is not sold.

When information may be shared

Access is limited to the Royal Coat team and service providers used to operate the website and business communications. When it is necessary to answer a technical or commercial request, relevant details may be shared with the represented manufacturer connected to that request. Royal Coat does not ask a manufacturer to use those details for an unrelated purpose.

Retention and security

Information is kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the enquiry, follow-up, business records or a legal obligation. Royal Coat takes reasonable steps to restrict access and protect the information in its possession.

LinkedIn publishing

Royal Coat may use an approved LinkedIn integration to publish Royal Coat articles and links to the existing Royal Coat Company Page. This website does not use that integration to collect LinkedIn profile information or read private LinkedIn messages.

Your choices

You may ask Royal Coat to correct or delete information you have provided, or ask the team to stop sending follow-up communication. Some records may need to be retained where required for legal or business record-keeping purposes.
